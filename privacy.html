Freqly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Freqly is a sound spectrum analyzer for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Short version: nothing leaves your phone.

Microphone

Freqly uses the microphone only while the Live screen is open, to measure the frequencies in the sound around you. The sound is analysed in memory on your phone, as it arrives, and then discarded. It is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

Files you open

When you open an audio or video file in the Recording screen, Android gives Freqly permission to read only that file. It is analysed on your phone. Freqly does not copy, upload, or keep it.

Exports

If you tap Share, Freqly creates an image or a CSV file of the spectrum on your phone and hands it to the app you choose (for example email or messaging). What happens after that is up to you and that app. Freqly deletes its temporary copies after a day.

What Freqly does not do

  • No internet access: the app does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No location, contacts, or other personal information is accessed.

Settings

Your settings (frequency range, averaging, RPM pulses, A4 reference, theme) and any curves you save are stored only on your phone and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

Freqly is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone.
